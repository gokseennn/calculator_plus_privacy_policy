Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Operator: Ahmet Göksen Akyıldız

Calculator Plus is a private vault that protects your photos, videos, contacts, and notes with a PIN. This policy explains what data we process and how. The app is published for iOS under bundle ID com.akyildiz.calculatorPlus.

1. Who we are

The data controller for Calculator Plus is Ahmet Göksen Akyıldız. For privacy questions, email ahmet.goksen81@icloud.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Your PIN is stored in the device secure area (iOS Keychain via encrypted storage). Vault content — photos, videos, contacts, and notes — stays in this app’s on-device storage. You do not create an account with us, and we do not host your PIN or vault files as a user account on our servers.

3. Permissions

  • Photos: used to import media into the vault and, if you choose, remove the original from the device gallery after import.
  • Camera and microphone: used to capture photos or videos for the vault.
  • Photo library add-only access: requested when you save a vault file back to Photos.

If you deny a permission, that feature will not work. Existing vault data remains on the device.

4. Advertising

The free version may show Google AdMob ads. Google may process technical data such as an advertising identifier, IP address, and device information to serve, measure, and limit ads. Lifetime Pro turns ads off. Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase through the Apple App Store (product ID com.calculatorplus.one_time_purchase). Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card details. Purchase status is stored on the device and can be restored with your Apple account. Apple’s policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Analytics

To operate and improve the app, we may send usage events (for example which screen or feature was used) to our analytics server as a simple key/value record. Photos you add to the vault may be sent in compressed form (JPEG / Base64) to that server. Videos are not sent this way. Analytics requests do not include your PIN, contacts, or notes.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Apple (App Store and payments) and Google (ads) may process data under their own policies. We do not share vault content with third parties except where required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device vault data remains until you delete items in the app or uninstall Calculator Plus. Uninstalling removes local vault files and the stored PIN from that device. Analytics records kept on our server are used only to operate and improve the product and are not used to build a public profile of you.

9. Children

Calculator Plus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

10. International transfers

Analytics and advertising partners may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

11. Your choices

  • You can deny Photos, Camera, or Microphone access in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete vault items inside the app or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • You can disable personalized ads in iOS tracking / privacy settings where available.
  • You can email us to ask what analytics we hold and to request deletion of server-side analytics tied to your submissions where we can identify them.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
